Who We Are

A Family Passion
on Four Wheels

Just Griddlin' started with a simple belief: great food doesn't need to be complicated. It needs to be made with care, fresh ingredients, and a whole lot of love. What began as a dream became a rolling kitchen that's become a staple at events and festivals all across Michigan.

Josh built this business from the ground up — from the bright lime green truck you can spot from a mile away, to every recipe on the menu. This isn't just a job. It's a calling. And every plate that leaves this window is a reflection of everything this family stands for.

"We don't just cook food — we make memories. Every event, every fair, every customer who comes back is why we do this."

— Josh, Founder

Forever in our hearts
